Product Selection

Application Fit

We match blend to menu. Kopi susu? FORGE. Multi-menu outlet? TORQUE. Premium positioning? VECTOR or PRIME. The right coffee for the right context.

Consistency Systems

Documented roasting profiles, batch tracking, and quality control. Every bag tastes like the last one. Your customers depend on it.

Workflow Support

Grinder calibration guidance, extraction parameters, and storage recommendations. We help your team execute consistently.

Custom Blend

YOUR BUSINESS ISN'T GENERIC.
WHY SHOULD YOUR COFFEE BE?

A signature blend developed exclusively for your brand. From discovery to production in 7 weeks.

Discover

30-min free consultation. We learn your business, target market, menu, and goals.

Develop

Market-fit analysis, flavor profile mapping, and formula design based on your inputs.

Test

Trial batch (Rp 500.000, 2–3 variations). Cupping session and feedback iteration.

Produce

Final formulation, production contract, and consistent supply. Minimum 5kg/month.

What You Get

  • ▪ Exclusive blend formulation for your brand
