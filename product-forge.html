For Business

The Problem It Solves

Many Arabica-heavy blends get lost in milk. The customer tastes mostly dairy and sugar, not coffee. FORGE's 70% Robusta base delivers the body and chocolate backbone needed to stay present in kopi susu, latte, and cappuccino.

The Menu Fit

Ideal for: es kopi susu (hero menu), kopi susu gula aren, cappuccino, latte, flat white. Any menu where milk is the dominant volume.

The Business Case

At Rp 175.000/kg wholesale with predictable 10% volume discounts, FORGE delivers consistent margin for high-volume milk-drink operations. The repeat customer rate improves when the coffee taste survives the milk.

For Your Cup

What It Tastes Like

Milk chocolate upfront, followed by hazelnut warmth and a brown sugar sweetness that lingers. The caramel note emerges as the cup cools. Low acidity means zero sharpness — just smooth, familiar satisfaction.

How to Brew It

Shines as espresso (1:2 ratio, 28–32 seconds) and in any milk-based preparation. Also works as a bold pour-over for those who want a heavier morning cup. Avoid very long extractions — the Robusta can turn bitter past 35 seconds.

Who Will Love It

The daily drinker who wants reliability over adventure. The home barista perfecting their latte art. Anyone who believes coffee should taste like coffee, even with milk.
