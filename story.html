Our Story

PEOPLE × COFFEE
× BUSINESS.

Three years. Twenty-plus partners. One belief: coffee should be built around the people and purpose it serves.

Why

WE EXIST TO BRIDGE WORLDS.

THREECORE was founded on a simple observation: the people who drink coffee, the people who serve it, and the people who build businesses around it rarely speak the same language.

We saw home brewers struggling to find fresh, honest beans. We saw coffee shop owners drowning in generic supplier pitches that ignored their actual business needs. We saw a gap between craft and commerce that nobody was filling.

So we built a roastery that serves both. Not as an afterthought. As a core philosophy.

WHY
HOW
Philosophy

COFFEE IS NEVER JUST COFFEE.

We believe coffee exists within a larger ecosystem. Someone drinks it. Someone serves it. Someone builds a business around it. And the coffee connects them.

This means we don't start with the bean and ask people to fit it. We start with the person, the purpose, and the business — then build the coffee experience around that.

"We don't simply start with coffee and ask people to fit it. We understand who the coffee is for, where it will be used, what it needs to accomplish, and then build the coffee experience around that purpose."

Craft

BUILT WITH CARE, BATCH AFTER BATCH.

Our roasting operation in Sidoarjo, Jawa Timur, runs on a simple non-negotiable: consistency. Every blend has a documented profile. Every batch is tracked. Every bag is vacuum-sealed to preserve freshness.

We don't chase certifications we haven't earned. We don't overhype origins. We do the work: select good beans, roast them carefully, package them honestly, and deliver them on time.

Partnership

NOT A SUPPLIER. A PARTNER.

In 3+ years of operation, we've learned that the best client relationships don't look like transactions. They look like collaborations.

We check in every two weeks. We announce new products before they launch. We invite clients into a private community where they can share tips, ask questions, and grow together.

Our retention rate target is 75%+ because we believe existing clients are 5x more valuable than new ones. We act accordingly.

People

WHO WE SERVE.

On the B2B side: kedai kopi susu independen, modern coffee shops, small chains (2–5 outlets), warkop modern, and small resellers across Jawa Timur.

On the B2C side: home brewer enthusiasts who own V60s and scales, casual premium drinkers who want better coffee without complexity, and gifters who value presentation and story.

Two different audiences. One shared need: coffee that makes sense for them.

Future

WHERE WE'RE GOING.

Year 1 (2026–2027) is about foundation: digital presence, B2C retail launch, community building, and consolidating our 20+ B2B relationships into 40–60 active accounts.

Year 2 brings subscription programs, curated brewing equipment, and the Threecore Academy. Year 3 explores flagship café concepts and direct trade relationships with farmers.

The 10-year vision: become the most relevant coffee brand in Indonesia for anyone serious about coffee — from home brewer to café chain.
